## How to copy files (Windows)

1. Open File Explorer  
2. Go to: `C:\Users\gardo\Vicky\website\catalogues\`  
3. Drop PDFs into `athangudi/pdfs/` or `other-catalogues/pdfs/`  
4. Drop photos into the matching hall folder under `product-photos/`  
5. In Cursor chat: “I dropped the Athangudi PDFs”
