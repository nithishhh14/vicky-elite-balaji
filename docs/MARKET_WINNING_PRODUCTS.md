# Winning products — what the market is asking for

_Researched 2026-09-17. Secondary research (market reports, trade guides,
competitor catalogues), not client sales data. Figures below are the
publishers' estimates, quoted for direction only. **None of them may be
published on the website as Elite Balaji claims.**_

## 1. The demand picture in five lines

1. **Coimbatore is building.** Home sales rose 36% in 2024, the fastest of
   India's 15 largest tier-2 cities, with a shift to premium, low-density
   independent villas; the Western Ring Road and airport expansion are opening
   new residential areas. [Radiance Realty](https://www.radiancerealty.in/blogs/coimbatore-real-estate-trends-2026-independent-villas-veerakeralam)
2. **South India is the fastest-growing tile region** (≈10.2% CAGR to 2031),
   and demand is moving up to large-format GVT/porcelain at a 20–30% price
   premium. [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/india-ceramic-tiles-market)
3. **Bathrooms are being modernised:** pedestal basins → over-counter basins,
   floor-mounted → wall-hung and rimless WCs, plus matte-black fittings,
   rain showers and frameless glass. [Maximize Market Research](https://www.maximizemarketresearch.com/market-report/indian-sanitary-ware-market/85262/) · [HomeLane](https://www.homelane.com/design-ideas/bathroom-design/9-bathroom-trends-that-will-dominate/)
4. **Stone is strong, and Indian stone is back in fashion:** granite holds
   ~41% of India's ornamental stone market (Tamil Nadu is a key source); warm
   neutrals and genuine Kota/Tandur/granite are trending in 2026 homes.
   [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6074644/india-construction-ornamental-stone-market) · [Studio Matrx](https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/flooring-trends-india-2026)
5. **Kitchens:** quartz and granite-composite sinks and quartz counters are
   the growth lines (quartz/composites ≈8.4% CAGR); buyers are leaving plain
   steel sinks. [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/india-kitchen-sink-and-other-related-markets) · [Lorazzo](https://lorazzo.com/blogs/blog/why-quartz-sinks-are-trending-in-2026-kitchens)

## 3. Where Elite Balaji can win (not just compete)

- **Villa package, one team:** granite counters + large-format floors + basins
  + adhesive + laying, starting with the **free site measurement**. Most
  competitors sell tiles only; nobody locally shows stone yard + crews + custom
  work together online.
- **Stone specialism:** real slab photos from our own yard beat catalogue
  pages. Keep adding real photos.
- **Custom stonecraft & printed tiles, pan-India:** no local competitor offers
  it online.

## 4. What to ask the client this week (turns research into catalogue)

1. Do you stock or source **wood-look plank tiles**? Which brands and sizes?
2. **800×1600 / 1200×2400 slabs** — which ranges?
3. **Wall-hung / rimless WCs and concealed cisterns** — which brands and models?
4. **Cool roof tiles, parking tiles, pavers, elevation tiles** — any of these?
5. **Fittings** (matte black, rain showers) and **glass partitions** — supplied or not?
6. Real photos: quartz sinks, Kota lots, any finished villa jobs.

Each confirmed "yes" becomes: product records (from their catalogue PDF, prices
stripped) → a landing page in `src/lib/guides.ts` → a Google Business Profile
post. Each "no" stays off the site.
